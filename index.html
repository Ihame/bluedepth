Meet Our Managing Director

The people behind BlueDepth's commitment to safety, professionalism, and excellence.

Tuyisenge Sulaiman

Managing Director & Co-Founder

One of three founders of BlueDepth Marine Group Ltd, Sulaiman brings more than 18 years of experience in operations management, business leadership, and commercial diving. He holds a Bachelor's Degree in Economics and Management and an MBA in Accounting and Finance, alongside PADI Open Water, PADI Advanced Open Water, and Helmet Diver certifications, pairing strong business expertise with hands-on technical experience.
